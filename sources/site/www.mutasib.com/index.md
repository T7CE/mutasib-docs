About Mutasib

## Smart Shop Management, Made Simple

Mutasib is a complete shop management platform, purpose-built for small and medium businesses. We started with a simple idea: **running your shop should be easy, fast, and always within reach — no expensive hardware or complex software required.**

From barcode-based inventory tracking and sales processing, to Analytics reports and customer management — Mutasib brings everything into one unified dashboard, accessible from your phone or computer.

We crafted the analytics dashboard with meticulous care: **interactive charts, revenue and net profit breakdowns, AI-powered demand forecasting**, and a polished interface with smooth animations that make tracking your shop's performance a delightful experience — not just rows of numbers.

We are **free to start**, with affordable plans starting at $7/month. No hidden fees, no long-term contracts, and no complex setup. Just sign up and start in minutes.
