## Getting started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Register at [mutasib.com/register](https://www.mutasib.com/register/) or call `POST /api/v1/auth/register`. Choose the **shopowner** role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your JWT token">
    Log in with `POST /api/v1/auth/login`. The response contains `access` and `refresh` tokens. Pass `Authorization: Bearer <access>` on every authenticated request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your shop">
    Call `POST /api/v1/shops/` with your shop name, address, and category. You'll receive a `shop_id` you'll use throughout the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
All API endpoints are prefixed with `https://api.mutasib.com`. JWT tokens expire periodically — use `POST /api/v1/auth/refresh` with your `refresh` token to get a new `access` token without logging in again.
</Note>
